Questions

Fair questions, honest answers

Is it really free?

Yes. The free version is a real app, not a teaser — you get full sessions, every breathing pattern, the sleep timer, reminders, favorites and the whole 10-day Foundations course, with no time limits and no account required. Premium unlocks the full library of sessions, scenes and sounds.

Can it really help with anxiety or panic?

Slow, steady breathing is a simple, well-known way to help your body wind down. Many people use Pocket Breath Coach to calm a racing mind, ease into sleep or steady themselves in a stressful moment. It isn't a medical treatment or a cure — think of it as a calming tool you can reach for whenever you need it.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. You can download and start breathing right away — no sign-up, no email, no card. An optional account only exists if you want to sync your favorites and reminders across devices.

How is it different from other meditation apps?

Most wellness apps are broad platforms built around courses and narrated content. Pocket Breath Coach does one thing: paced, slow breathing — with precise control over your rhythm, and visuals and sound that follow it. There's nothing to keep up with, it works offline, and it's inexpensive.

Which breathing techniques are included?

Box breathing, 4-7-8, coherent (around six breaths a minute), calming long-exhale ratios like 1:2 and 4:6, pranayama-inspired patterns and vagus-nerve resets — over 260 presets in all. Every one is adjustable down to a tenth of a second, and you can save your own.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Free sessions work entirely offline, and Premium lets you download the full sound library to your phone. Airplane mode is arguably the best way to use it.

What do you do with my data?

As little as possible. There are no ads, no third-party trackers and no analytics — we deliberately removed them. Your favorites and history stay on your device unless you create an account to sync them. See the privacy policy for details.
